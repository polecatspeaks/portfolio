I took a few months off from developing [STAR](/projects). Not because I lost interest; life kind of happened.

I was laid off, and I spent that time getting a lot of work done on STAR. It was a really productive summer. Then I got hired, which is good. Yay, we like money. But as a DevOps guy, the first six months in a new shop are overwhelming: you drink straight from the fire hose all day, every day. I'm not here to complain about the new job, though. I'm genuinely thankful for it.

## Where STAR is today

We've done a lot of reworking of the rules so that STAR, and Ruth (what I call STAR's voice), get the right answers to your questions.

Just as important, we're working to make sure that when STAR makes a proactive call, it's correct. My theory: no proactive call beats a bad proactive call, and no data beats bad data. So that's where the tightening is happening.

## What's next

I think I might be ready to possibly, hopefully, maybe release version 1.0 to the public in Q2 next year. We'll see.

Until then, this is just a quick update. We'll keep you posted. Thanks for reading.
